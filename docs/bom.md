## Camera mechanical notes

- PCB 32 × 32 × 6.5 mm.
- 4× M2 holes — measure actual pitch with calipers before Onshape. Do not trust the listing.
- Leave ~8–10 mm in front of the board so the M12 barrel can still turn.
- Cable exits aft. Strain-relief in the head shell, not on the connector.
